**Title (30 max):** Salt & Sail: Pirate Seas

**Short description (80 max):** Chart a fog-wrapped archipelago, chase legends, and sail by your own stars.

**Full description:**

Cast off into a sea nobody has mapped.

Salt & Sail is a short-voyage pirate adventure played with one thumb. Steer
your ship across a fresh, hand-inked archipelago every voyage. Fog lifts as
you sail, and what hides behind it is up to the weather: glittering shallows,
lonely lighthouses, drowned wrecks, whirlpools, skull-shaped rocks and lights
that only appear after dark.

- One-thumb sailing: drag anywhere, the ship follows your stick.
- A living sea: clear skies, fog, rain, storms and night, each with its own
  look, sound and music. Lightning, whitecaps and lantern light are all real-time.
- Fog of war: every voyage starts blank. Your chart fills in as you explore.
- Places to find: harbour towns trade rumours, lighthouses clear the mist,
  treasure islets hide chests, wrecks hold salvage, bottles hold clues.
- Voyages that fit a coffee break: five to ten minutes.
- No ads. No tracking. No network needed.
