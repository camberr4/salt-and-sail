# Design summary

The full design lives in the "Pirate Sea Game: Design Doc" on claude.ai. This is a short copy so sessions can start from the repo.

- Pitch: top-down pirate roguelite, fantasy archipelago, 5 to 10 minute voyages.
- Orientation: portrait only (decided at milestone 1), played one-handed. The world is 10 by 10 portrait screens (10800x19200 units).
- Controls: floating on-screen joystick (touch anywhere); ship turns toward the stick, pushing further adds speed.
- Sea: about 10 by 10 screens, zoomed-out camera, something to see within a few seconds of sailing.
- Encounters: text events with choices; ship combat with 2 to 3 second tap mini-games (Broadside, Evade, Repair, Flee).
- Weather: clear, fog, rain, storm, night, with matching music states.
- Goal: one legend per voyage plus three side goals; Atlas fills across runs.
- Ship: six stats (Hull, Supplies, Sails, Cannons, Hold, Spyglass), visible upgrades, livery.
- Monetization: free for three voyages, then a one-time unlock.
